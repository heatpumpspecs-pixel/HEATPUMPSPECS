Domestic heat pump specifications

Clear technical data. Backed by evidence.

HEATPUMPSPECS is an evidence-based technical specifications database for domestic heat pumps, focused on the Irish and European market. Our aim is to make product information easier to compare, verify and trace back to authoritative sources.

Project foundation

Building the catalogue

The catalogue is being developed around structured product records, manufacturer documentation and certification evidence, including CEN KEYMARK and Eurovent where relevant.

Specifications will retain their source references and operating conditions. Certification information will be presented separately from grant or installation eligibility.

Product records and the searchable catalogue will be added as research and verification progress.

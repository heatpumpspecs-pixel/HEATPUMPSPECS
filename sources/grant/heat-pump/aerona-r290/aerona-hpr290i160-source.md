## 6. Source differences retained for review

The current product page reports depth 419 mm, while the family technical-data sheet reports 425 mm for HPR290i160. The current product page reports weight 182/210 kg, while the family sheet labels 180/184 kg as empty/full. These weight labels are not equivalent definitions and are not reconciled here.

The current product page reports SCOP 35°C of 4.59; the family technical-data sheet reports 4.50 at 35°C and 3.29 at 55°C. The model-specific Ecodesign datasheet reports ηs values rather than SCOP. These values must not be treated as interchangeable. The matching KEYMARK subtype page reports SCOP 4.63 (low temperature) and 3.4 (medium temperature). All source statements are retained separately pending clarification of document revision and method.

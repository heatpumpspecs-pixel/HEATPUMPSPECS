## 2. Scheme scope — distinction from domestic HARP and grant eligibility

SEAI's “Energy efficient products” page distinguishes two separate databases:
- the Triple E Register, described as a searchable list of energy-efficient products, typically with a best-in-class efficiency standard; and
- HARP, described as the product-efficiency database for home-heating appliances.

SEAI's Triple E Register page states that the register contains products meeting minimum energy-efficiency criteria, typically best-in-class, and that businesses selecting listed equipment can use the Accelerated Capital Allowance (ACA) tax incentive.

Therefore, the HPU117632 listing is evidence that the exact Grant product code appears in SEAI's **Triple E / ACA product register**. It is not evidence by itself of HARP listing, nor does it establish domestic SEAI heat-pump grant approval.

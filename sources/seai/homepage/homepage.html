SEAI Energy Awards 2026

Winners Announced

The SEAI Energy Awards celebrate excellence in pursuit of cleaner, more affordable and secure energy. Award entrants in 2026 collectively saved €25 million in energy costs. 

Caroline Ashe Brady received the coveted Chair’s Award for Outstanding Contribution to Sustainable Energy. Caroline was recognised for her leadership in advancing sustainability and innovation in the construction sector.
